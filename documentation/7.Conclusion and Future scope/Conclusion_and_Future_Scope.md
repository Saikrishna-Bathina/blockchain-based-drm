# Chapter 7: Conclusion and Future Scope

## 7.1 Conclusion
The **Blockchain-Based Digital Rights Management System** successfully mitigates conventional pitfalls encountered in centralized Intellectual Property (IP) governance. By establishing a robust computational **Originality Engine**, the system comprehensively filters plagiarized or duplicated intellectual properties accurately predicting the authenticity of dynamic components across Images, Audio, Video, and Text. 

Furthermore, merging advanced machine learning uniqueness verification seamlessly with Ethereum-based **Smart Contracts** formulates a self-sustaining decentralized economy supporting direct creator royalties devoid of excessive intermediary interference. The secure content delivery mechanism (JWT and AES encryption streams) guarantees that high-definition digital items remain cryptographically locked away from unauthorized scraping bots.

---

## 7.2 Future Scope
While the current framework ensures state-of-the-art protection against generalized copyright infringements, future developmental trajectories possess extensive enhancements specifically:
1. **Generative AI Protection Protocols:** Further adjusting SBERT parameters uniquely isolating generative AI watermarks and language structures to automatically classify AI-generated content definitively separating them from human artistry.
2. **Cross-Chain Bridging:** Permitting seamless transfers and verifications natively between varied blockchains such as Polygon, Avalanche, or Solana minimizing gas costs extensively while increasing ecosystem availability.
3. **Zero-Knowledge Oracles (zk-SNARKs):** While currently avoided due to stream latency, optimizing cryptographic privacy for creators opting strictly to retain asset contents globally unidentifiable publicly while simultaneously verifying their specific license allocations directly via off-chain zk-Rollups.
